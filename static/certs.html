Certificates

The TLS certificates this machine serves: the panel's own, Let's Encrypt, and the ones nginx points to. Read only: no private key is ever read, and nothing here renews or changes a certificate. An expiring certificate also appears on the Alerts page.

A certificate the panel made for itself is marked self-signed: browsers warn about it until you trust it, which is expected. Let's Encrypt certificates are normally renewed automatically about 30 days before they end.
