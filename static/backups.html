Backups

Saves copies of the folders you choose, on a schedule or on demand. Copies are stored in a place only the administrator (root) can read: this machine, a network share or a disk mounted under /mnt.

Settings

To store copies on another computer, add a Windows (SMB) share for backups on the Network shares page. To use another disk, such as an iSCSI disk, connect, format and mount it on the Storage page (it must appear under /mnt); it is then listed here.

A copy on the same machine does not protect you from losing the machine or its disk: store copies on a network share, or copy the files from /var/backups/adminpanel somewhere else too. Not saved: /proc, /sys, /dev, /run and other mounted disks inside a folder.
