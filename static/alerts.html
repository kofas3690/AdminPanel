Alerts

Problems found on this machine right now: a disk filling up, memory running out, and failed services. The list refreshes by itself, and the number also shows in the top bar on every page.

Settings

Muted services

A muted service is not counted or shown above. Use this for a service you know is failing and have decided to ignore.

Email

Send a message when a new problem appears and when it clears. Checked every two minutes while the panel runs. The connection must be encrypted. For Gmail and similar, use an app password, not your normal password.
